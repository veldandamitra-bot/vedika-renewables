Vedika Renewables is building an integrated clean-energy ecosystem across renewable energy, energy storage, green fuels, advanced manufacturing and sustainable infrastructure — creating scalable solutions for a cleaner and energy-independent India.

Solutions

One partner across
generation, storage and consumption.

Solar

High-performance solar panel systems for homes, businesses and industry — rooftop, carport and ground-mount arrays engineered for Indian irradiance, tariffs and long-term yield.

BESS — Battery Energy Storage

Battery energy storage systems (BESS) that shift renewable output into peak hours, protect critical loads and cut diesel backup — core green-energy infrastructure alongside solar.

Green Hydrogen

Electrolysis-based green hydrogen production powered entirely by renewable energy — supplying clean fuel for industry, transport and grid balancing as India scales its hydrogen economy.

Green Ammonia

Carbon-free ammonia synthesised from green hydrogen and renewable nitrogen — a critical feedstock for fertilisers, industrial chemicals and next-generation clean shipping fuel.

Compressed Biogas (CBG)

Biomass-to-CBG plants converting agricultural waste, municipal solid waste and press mud into pipeline-quality biogas — a circular energy solution aligned with India's SATAT programme.

Green Energy Generation & Make in India

Building India's clean energy
future — from the ground up.

India stands at the most consequential moment in its energy history. The transition to clean energy is no longer a distant ambition — it is a national imperative, and the opportunity to lead it from within India has never been greater.

Green Energy Generation

From utility-scale solar and wind to green hydrogen, green ammonia and compressed biogas — Vedika Renewables is building a diversified clean energy generation platform that spans every node of India's energy transition. Our projects are designed for scale, bankability and long-term performance, contributing directly to India's 500 GW renewable target.

Make in India — Solar Manufacturing

True energy independence begins with manufacturing sovereignty. Vedika Renewables is committed to building India's domestic solar supply chain — from ingot pulling and wafer slicing to high-efficiency cell production. By manufacturing at home, we reduce import dependence, create skilled employment and ensure that India's solar boom generates lasting industrial value within its borders.

For India, From India

Every project we build, every MW we commission and every component we manufacture is a deliberate act of nation-building. We design for Indian irradiance, build for Indian infrastructure constraints, and price for Indian economics — because the energy transition only succeeds if it works for every segment of India, not just the most profitable ones.

"The energy transition is not just an environmental project — it is India's greatest industrial opportunity. We intend to seize it."
