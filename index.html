Solutions

One partner across
generation, storage and consumption.

Solar

High-performance solar panel systems for homes, businesses and industry — rooftop, carport and ground-mount arrays engineered for Indian irradiance, tariffs and long-term yield.

BESS — Battery Energy Storage

Battery energy storage systems (BESS) that shift renewable output into peak hours, protect critical loads and cut diesel backup — core green-energy infrastructure alongside solar.

Green Hydrogen

Electrolysis-based green hydrogen production powered entirely by renewable energy — supplying clean fuel for industry, transport and grid balancing as India scales its hydrogen economy.

Green Ammonia

Carbon-free ammonia synthesised from green hydrogen and renewable nitrogen — a critical feedstock for fertilisers, industrial chemicals and next-generation clean shipping fuel.

Compressed Biogas (CBG)

Biomass-to-CBG plants converting agricultural waste, municipal solid waste and press mud into pipeline-quality biogas — a circular energy solution aligned with India's SATAT programme.

Our approach

Measure first.
Design second.
Install once.

Every engagement starts with data: twelve months of bills, a load profile and a physical site survey. From there we model generation, quantify savings and only then recommend a system size. Efficiency measures are considered before capacity is added, because reducing demand is almost always cheaper than generating more.

After commissioning, monitoring keeps the asset honest — deviations from the modelled curve trigger a service visit rather than a surprise at the end of the year.
